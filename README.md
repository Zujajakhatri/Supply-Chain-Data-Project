# Pakistan Supply Chain Finance (SCF) Opportunity Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes **Supply Chain Finance (SCF) opportunities across districts of Pakistan**, with a focus on SME activity, dominanat value chains, anchor sectors, production capacity, trade orientation, banking access, financing gaps, and digital readiness.

The analysis identifies districts and dominanat industries with **High, Medium, and Low SCF potential** and maps them to relevant banking products.

## Objectives

* Identify high-potential industries and value chains for SCF.
* Assess SME and production activity across districts.
* Evaluate banking penetration and access to credit.
* Measure working capital and financing gaps.
* Analyze export orientation and trade-finance potential.
* Recommend suitable SCF products for different business ecosystems.

## Key Indicators

The dataset includes indicators such as:

* Estimated SMEs and employees
* Production volume and processing capacity
* Dominant industries and value chains
* Anchor-firm presence
* Infrastructure and logistics connectivity
* Banking penetration
* Access to credit/lending
* Working capital gap
* Export orientation
* Trade facilitation
* Digital readiness

## Opportunity Scoring

The **SCF Opportunity Score** combines four major dimensions:

| Dimension                           | Weight |
| ----------------------------------- | -----: |
| Supply Chain Potential              |    25% |
| Financing Need                      |    25% |
| Trade Finance Potential             |    20% |
| Digital Readiness                   |    15% |
| Export and trade facilitation       |    15% |

Opportunity levels are classified as:

* **Low:** 0–60
* **Medium:** 61–80
* **High:** 81–100

## Recommended SCF Products

Based on district characteristics and value-chain requirements, the analysis maps opportunities to products such as:

* Distributor / Channel Finance
* Purchase Order Finance
* Payables Finance
* Receivables Discounting
* Inventory Finance
* Factoring
